Privacy policy

Last updated 26 November 2026

This policy explains what personal information KizicTech collects through this website and through our apps, and what we do with it.

Who we are

KizicTech Pty Ltd (ABN 47 681 167 948), referred to here as "KizicTech", "we" or "us", is an Australian company based in New South Wales that researches and develops AI-powered apps and products. This policy covers kizictech.com.au and the apps and products we publish. You can reach us by sending an enquiry.

This website

The website has no accounts, no advertising and no analytics or tracking cookies. We don't ask for personal information when you browse it. The only place you can give us any is the enquiry form.

  • Hosting. As with any website, our hosting provider keeps standard server logs, which can include your IP address, browser type and the pages requested. These are used to keep the site running and secure.
  • Fonts. The site loads its typeface from Google Fonts, so your browser makes a request to Google's servers when a page loads. Google's handling of that request is covered by Google's privacy policy.

When you send an enquiry

The enquiry form asks for your name, your email address and your message, and optionally the name of your business. We use these details to reply and to resolve your request, and keep them only as long as that takes or as the law requires.

The form is delivered to our inbox by Web3Forms, a form-handling service. It receives what you enter in order to pass it on to us, and its handling of that information is covered by its privacy policy. Our inbox is provided by Google.

Our apps

Each app we publish collects only the information it needs to work. The specific data an app collects, and why, is disclosed on that app's App Store or Google Play listing and inside the app before you use it. Where an app handles data in a way this policy doesn't describe, the app's own privacy notice applies alongside this one.

Apps we publish may use AI features. Where an app sends your content to an AI service to produce a result, the app tells you so, and the content is used to provide that feature to you.

What we don't do

  • We don't sell personal information.
  • We don't share personal information with advertisers.
  • We don't use personal information for purposes unrelated to the product you're using.

Sharing information

We share personal information only with service providers that help us run our website and apps, such as hosting, email and AI service providers, and only as far as they need it to do that job. Some of these providers store or process information outside Australia. We may also disclose information where the law requires it.

Security

We take reasonable steps to protect personal information from misuse, loss and unauthorised access. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we can't guarantee absolute security.

Children

Our website and apps are made for businesses and adults. We don't knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has given us personal information, send us an enquiry and we'll delete it.

Your rights

You can ask to see the personal information we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it. Send us an enquiry and we'll respond within a reasonable time. If you're in Australia and aren't satisfied with our response, you can contact the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner.

Changes to this policy

When we change this policy we'll post the new version here and update the date at the top.

Contact

Questions about privacy? Send us an enquiry.
