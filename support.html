Support

Help with any KizicTech app or product comes from one place: send us an enquiry and a person will read it.

Get support

Send us an enquiry. To help us sort it out quickly, include:

  • The name of the app or product
  • Your device and its operating system version, for example iPhone on iOS 26
  • What you were trying to do, and what happened instead
  • Whether you have a screenshot. The form can't take attachments, so we'll ask for it when we reply

Your account and data

To ask what data an app holds about you, or to have your account and data deleted, send us an enquiry and enter the email address you use with the app, so we can find your account. The privacy policy explains how we handle personal information.

Purchases and refunds

Purchases made through the App Store or Google Play are billed by Apple or Google, and refund requests go through them:

  • Request a refund from Apple
  • Request a refund from Google Play

If a purchase isn't working as it should, send us an enquiry as well so we can fix the cause.

Feedback

Suggestions and bug reports are welcome too: send us an enquiry.
